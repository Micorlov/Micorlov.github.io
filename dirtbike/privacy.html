Dirtbike: Offline Motocross — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Micorlov develops Dirtbike: Offline Motocross. This policy describes the current offline Android game. Contact us at micorlov@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information in the game

The current game does not collect or share personal data with the developer or third parties. It does not require an account and does not show advertising, offer in-app purchases, or use analytics tracking or remote crash-reporting services.

Your career progress, stars, unlocked bikes, tuning, cosmetic choices, lap records, best-lap ghost replays, and settings are saved locally on your device to provide gameplay. These records are not sent to us. Device backup and restoration may depend on your Android settings and your device or backup provider.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled by our email provider. We retain support correspondence only as needed to provide support and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us.

Storage, security, and deletion

Local game records are stored within the application's device storage, protected by Android's access controls. No storage or communication method is completely secure.

You can delete local game records through Android Settings, Apps, Dirtbike, Storage, Clear data. This permanently resets local progress. Copies retained by your device backup provider are controlled through that provider's settings. We cannot access or delete your device's local records remotely.

Young players

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older, subject to any higher regional age rating. It does not collect personal information from anyone. It has no chat, accounts, advertising, or purchases, and it does not ask for a name, email address, or location.

A parent or guardian with questions about a child's use of the game can contact us at the email address above.

This website

This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary website request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page contains no advertising or analytics tracking.

Changes

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date. If future versions introduce advertising, purchases, online services, or different data practices, this policy and the game's disclosures will be updated before those versions are released.
