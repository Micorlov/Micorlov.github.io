Dirt Bike Florida – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026. Developer: Michael Orlov ("Micorlov"). App: Dirt Bike Florida (com.micorlov.dirtbike). Contact: micorlov@gmail.com.

1. Summary

The game has no accounts. Your career, settings and records are stored on your device. The free game shows ads served by Google AdMob. Optional usage statistics are off unless you turn them on.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner and interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for cosmetic rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interactions, to deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised only if you consent. Elsewhere, ad personalisation follows your Android settings. Rewarded ads in the game are requested as non-personalised. See Google's advertising policies.

3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent before ads load. Ads and usage statistics wait for your answer. You can change your choice any time with the "AD PRIVACY OPTIONS" button in Settings, which appears where required. Outside these regions no consent dialog is shown.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics 4)

Usage statistics are optional and off by default; you enable them in Settings. If enabled (and, in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, if consent has been given), the game sends to Google Analytics: a random client ID generated on your device, your IP address (received by Google when the data is sent), and events such as game opened and race results (course, mode, bike, finishing position and time). No name, email or account is used. Turning usage statistics off in Settings stops sending and deletes the random ID from your device.

5. Purchases

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase confirmation only; we never see your payment details.

6. Sharing

We share data only with Google (AdMob, Analytics, Google Play) as described above. We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device; uninstall the game to remove it. Data held by Google services is kept under Google's retention policies. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your random ID, email micorlov@gmail.com with the ID shown to us on request, or turn statistics off and use Google's ad settings and Android "Delete advertising ID" controls.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the email above.

9. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where a higher age applies).

10. Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.
