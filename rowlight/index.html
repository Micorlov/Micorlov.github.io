HOW TO PLAY

A simple goal.

  1. Tap or swipe a tile next to the empty square.
  2. Arrange the numbers from left to right, top to bottom. Leave the empty square at the bottom right.
  3. A small check means a tile is in place. An outlined tile can move.

On a keyboard, focus the board and use arrow keys to move the empty square. Press Space to pause.

The timer starts with your first move. Every shuffle is solvable, and there’s no countdown.
