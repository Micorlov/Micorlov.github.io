Off Road Miami — Privacy Policy (iPhone)

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Off Road Miami iPhone app on the App Store, published by Ayelet Orlov. The game is played offline. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress, jeeps, upgrades, records, ghost runs, badges and settings are saved only on your iPhone. Settings → Privacy → Delete my data in the game erases them. Deleting the app also deletes them.

Photos

Photo mode can save your shots to your photo library. iOS asks for permission to add photos the first time. The game only adds pictures; it cannot read your library.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The iPhone app does not send your data to the developer or to third parties.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
