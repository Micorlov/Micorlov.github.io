Off Road Miami — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Who we are

Off Road Miami (Android package com.micorlov.mudrockcrawler, previously named Mud & Rock Crawler and Offroad Florida) is made by Orlov Games, the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: micorlov@gmail.com. We answer privacy requests within one month.

Summary

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. It shows ads and uses Google services for statistics and crash reports. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, these services only store or read information on your device after you agree in the consent form shown by the game. You can change your choice at any time, and you can erase your data from inside the game.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Google as a recipient

Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited receive the data above. For Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) Google processes data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. For ads, Google also uses data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. With your consent, Google's ad technology partners listed in the consent form may also receive ad data.

Some data is processed in the United States. Google is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Analytics: user-level event data is deleted automatically after the retention period set in our Google Analytics property, at most 14 months.
  • Crashlytics: crash traces and installation IDs are kept for 90 days.
  • AdMob: according to Google's retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Emails to us: only as long as needed to handle your request.

Data kept only on your device

Your progress, jeeps, upgrades, records, ghost runs, badges and settings are saved on your phone and are never sent to us. Photos you take in photo mode go to your phone's gallery. Android backup is turned off for the game.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw consent: Settings → Privacy → Ad choices in the game (shown where consent applies). Withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place.
  • Erase your data: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data. This deletes your local progress, resets the Analytics app-instance ID (so earlier statistics can no longer be linked to your phone), discards unsent crash reports and resets your consent. You can also reset your Android advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Because the game has no accounts, we hold no name or email and usually cannot identify your records. Email us and we will help, for example by deleting data linked to an app-instance ID you send us.
  • You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. A list for the EEA is at edpb.europa.eu.

Permissions

The game asks for no runtime permissions. It declares Internet and network-state access (ads, statistics, crash reports), the advertising-ID permission (ads and statistics), and vibration (haptics, which you can turn off in Settings).

Young players

The game is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. If you are under the age at which you can give consent yourself in your country (between 13 and 16 in the EU), please ask a parent or guardian to make the choice in the consent form, or choose not to consent. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

We publish changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change needs your consent, the game will ask again.
