Learn Blackjack Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Applies to the Learn Blackjack Android app (com.blackjackcoach.app), formerly "Blackjack Coach"

In short: Learn Blackjack is a play-money blackjack trainer. There is no sign-up, no ads, no purchases and no real-money gambling. Your lessons stay on your phone. The app sends only anonymous usage stats and crash reports (which you can switch off) and, for the leaderboards, your score with a nickname.

1 Who this policy covers

This policy explains what Learn Blackjack collects, why, and who it's shared with. "We," "us," and "our" refer to the app's developer. If you have questions this page doesn't answer, see Contact us below.

2 Information we collect

Learn Blackjack has no accounts and no sign-in screen. We don't collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or files, and we don't use the advertising ID.

The nickname is generated by the app (for example "Calm Heron 42"). You may change it; please don't use your real name. Analytics events never contain your nickname or any text you type. Your chips are play money and never count toward any score.

3 Advertising

Learn Blackjack has no ads. It does not contain an ad SDK, removes the advertising-ID permission, and turns off ad personalization and Android ID collection in Firebase.

4 Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties. Google processes the data above for us as a service provider (How Google uses data from apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy). Your nickname and score are visible to other players on the leaderboards.

5 Your choices, retention and deletion

  • Turn off usage stats and crash reports: menu → Share anonymous usage stats. It stops immediately and stays off.
  • Retention: Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months; Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days; leaderboard entries are kept as the boards' history.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device, the installation ID and the anonymous sign-in. To have your leaderboard entries removed, email micorlov@gmail.com with your nickname and the dates you played; we'll delete them within 30 days.

6 Children's privacy

Learn Blackjack is intended for adults learning blackjack basic strategy and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7 Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Leaderboard data is protected by server rules: each player can write only their own entry, and nobody else can change it. Data on your device depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, OS updates).

8 Changes to this policy

If Learn Blackjack's data practices change, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above.

9 Contact us

Questions about this policy or how Learn Blackjack handles data can be sent to: micorlov@gmail.com
